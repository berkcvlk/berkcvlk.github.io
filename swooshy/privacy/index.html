Privacy Policy

Swooshy: Arrow Rush · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what data the Swooshy: Arrow Rush mobile game processes, why, and what rights you have. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

Data controller

Berk Civelek (individual). Contact: berkcvlk@gmail.com

We never see your payment details; Google Play or the App Store handles payment. Your settings, scores and progress are also stored on your phone.

Ads

The game only has rewarded ads, and they only play when you choose to watch one. In regions such as the European Economic Area and the UK a consent form is shown first. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission; if you decline, the game works the same and ads are not personalized. You can change your choices in your phone's settings.

Legal bases

We process data to provide the game (performance of a contract), to improve and secure it (legitimate interests), and, where required, with your consent (personalized ads, tracking).

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the service providers above, as needed to provide the service. Google and RevenueCat may process data on servers outside Turkey and the EU, under their contractual safeguards. Details: Google, RevenueCat.

Retention and deletion

Your cloud save is kept until you delete it. In the game, go to Settings → Delete account to instantly delete your account, cloud save and the save on your phone; this cannot be undone and also removes coins you bought. After that, the game runs without an account on this install. If you cannot access the game, write to berkcvlk@gmail.com with your Google Play Games or Game Center player name and we will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling only removes the save on your phone. Purchase records (RevenueCat, Google Play, App Store) may be kept longer for legal obligations. Only transaction numbers, which contain no personal information, stay on your phone so the same purchase is not granted twice.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you can access your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. Send requests to berkcvlk@gmail.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you notice this has happened, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Only your own account can access your cloud save, and data travels over encrypted connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the date above is updated.
